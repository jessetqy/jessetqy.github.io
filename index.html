Client outreach system

Prudential · internal

Designed and built independently · 2026

Given an assigned contact base, I wrote myself a proper set of reference material rather than working the list on instinct: opening questions that start from the person's situation instead of the product, a map of the objections that actually recur, and a follow-up cadence.

What I don't do is read from it. I go through the material before a call and then let the conversation go where it goes — a script you recite is audible down the phone, and it costs you the second conversation. The preparation is for me, not for them.

The same applies to language. I match how the person I'm speaking to actually speaks, which across clients in Penang and Kuala Lumpur is rarely corporate English.

Opening questions · objection map · follow-up cadence · reference notes by segment
